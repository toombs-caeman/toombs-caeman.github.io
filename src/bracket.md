My family does march madness a little differently than most.
This is mostly because most of us don't give a hoot about basketball, so a traditional bracket would leave most of us 'out of the running' for most of the tournament.
Instead, players can make their selection for each game individually, right up until the start of each game. Games in the first round (and play-in games) are worth one point, the second round is worth two points, the third three and so on. This makes it so that there are almost always enough points left that anyone could make a comeback. It keeps the tension high.

We track this all through a spreadsheet, since no one cares enough to cheat anyway.
